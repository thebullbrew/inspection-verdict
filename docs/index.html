The method

  1. Sort before you sum. Adding a $20k kitchen refresh to a $15k sewer line hides the emergency inside the wishlist.
  2. Price the mid, pad the must-fix. Contractors find friends behind walls. A 15% contingency is not pessimism, it is experience.
  3. Ask for credit, not repairs. Seller repairs are done cheap and fast. A credit lets your contractor do it right.
  4. Reprice, don't panic. Most bad inspections are not walk-aways — they are price corrections waiting for a calm buyer.
